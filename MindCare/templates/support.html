🆘 Support & Resources

MindCare is a self-care tool, not a crisis service or medical provider. If you're going through a difficult time, here are some options:

  • Talk to someone you trust — a friend, family member, or colleague.
  • Contact a qualified mental health professional or your doctor.
  • If you may be in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number right away.

Some starting points

In the United States, you can call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or text HOME to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line. If you're outside the US, a web search for "crisis helpline" plus your country's name can point you to a local, confidential service.

Automated analysis in this app can make mistakes and is never a substitute for professional judgment or emergency services.
